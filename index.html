Nathan mail sorter

A private tool that sorts its owner's own Gmail inbox. It reads new mail and files routine items (receipts, marketing, automated notices) out of the inbox under labels, so the inbox holds only mail that needs a person.

It has one user: its owner. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What it does

  • Reads message headers and content in the owner's mailbox to decide where each email belongs.
  • Adds and removes Gmail labels, including removing the Inbox label to archive. Every change is reversible.
  • Never sends, deletes or trashes mail.
